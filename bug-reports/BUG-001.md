## Preconditions
User `problem_user` / `secret_sauce` exists.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open https://www.saucedemo.com
2. Log in as `problem_user`
3. Look at the product images on the Products page

## Expected result
Each of the 6 products shows its own product image.

## Actual result
All products show the same image (a dog picture).
